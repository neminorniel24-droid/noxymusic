Connection help: friends on different networks

Phone hotspots and many home connections block direct links. A relay passes the music through the middle so anyone can join. A free shared relay is used automatically. For the most reliable party, make your own free account at a TURN provider such as Metered, copy its credentials link and paste it here. Everyone in the room should add it.
