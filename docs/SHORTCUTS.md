# Keyboard shortcuts

## Sound effects
Keys 1 to 7 trigger air horn, siren, laser, scratch, boing, bass drop and applause.

## DJ
Space plays or pauses the louder deck. The [ and ] keys move the crossfader. T runs the chosen transition.

## Studio and recording
M toggles the metronome and R starts or stops recording.

## Help
Press ? to open the help dialog and Escape to close it.

## Lights
Press L for lights-only mode and Escape to leave it.
