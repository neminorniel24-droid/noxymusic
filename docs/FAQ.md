# FAQ

## Do I need an account?
No. Open the site and start playing.

## Is my camera video uploaded?
No. Everything runs locally in your browser.

## Can I use my own songs?
Yes. Upload them in the DJ tab. They stay on your device.

## Why does the key change when I upload a song?
The app detects the song's key and sets your scale to match, so your notes sound right. You can change it any time.

## Which browsers work?
Chrome and Edge on desktop work best. Safari does not support the video filters.

## Can I connect several laptops?
Yes. Use the Party tab: one laptop creates a room, the others join with its 4-character code, and they all play the same songs, lights and effects. See docs/PARTY.md.

## Who controls the music in a party room?
Whoever presses play, uploads a song or starts a transition. The other laptops stop and follow.

## Can laptops in different cities or networks join one room?
Yes, with the room code. Most connect directly. For strict networks add a free relay under Party, Connection help. See docs/PARTY.md.

## Can friends see each other?
Yes, optionally. In Party, turn on Share my video. It is small, low quality and off by default.
